# Frontier 2026 — Judge Vision Deep Dive

## 🏛️ ANATOLY YAKOVENKO (Cofounder, Solana)

### The "Internet Capital Markets" (ICM) Thesis

**Core vision**: Solana = decentralized backbone for **Internet Capital Markets** (on-chain Nasdaq)

**3 pillars Solana's roadmap**:
1. Increase bandwidth ✅
2. Reduce latency ✅
3. **Address market microstructure** ← current focus

### 2026 Technical Priorities

**Application-Controlled Execution (ACE)**:
- Smart contracts get **millisecond-level control** over their own transaction ordering
- Critical for fair markets, MEV reduction
- Supports things like sealed-bid auctions, fair launches, private order books

**Alpenglow upgrade** (early 2026):
- Time-to-inclusion: **50-100ms**
- Finality: **~150ms**

**APE (Asynchronous Program Execution)** — post-Alpenglow

### Stablecoin Vision
- Predicts **$1T total stablecoin supply by 2026**
- Stablecoins central to global financial change
- Solana as primary settlement rail

### What Anatoly votes for in hackathon

Projects that:
- Demonstrate **ICM primitives** (price discovery, settlement, market microstructure)
- Use **Application-Controlled Execution** patterns
- Show stablecoin native UX
- Push **technical envelope** (use new primitives like Alpenglow features)
- Are **decade-long** infrastructure plays, not features

## 🌍 JUDGE GROUPS — Strategic Voting Patterns

### Group 1: Foundation Leadership (Anatoly, Lily, Jed, Scott)
- **Care about**: Long-term ecosystem impact, ICM thesis, technical depth
- **Vote for**: Infrastructure plays, stablecoin native UX, ACE/MPC patterns, decade vision
- **Skeptical of**: Toy demos, missing business model, no decade vision

### Group 2: Colosseum Team (Clay, Matty, Nate, Max, Michael)
- **Care about**: Startup viability, accelerator readiness, founder grit
- **Vote for**: Working demo + traction + clear monetization + founder-market fit
- **Skeptical of**: Unbuildable scope, weak founder narratives

### Group 3: Superteam Regional (Aditya, Cap, Carlo, Kuka, Patricia, Shek)
- **Care about**: Regional ecosystem health, mass-market accessibility
- **Vote for**: Apps with clear regional GTM, multi-language support, emerging market fit
- **Skeptical of**: US-centric assumptions, ignoring local payment methods

### Group 4: Sponsor Reps (Phantom, Arcium, Reflect, Squads, Metaplex, Raydium)
- **Care about**: Their tech being used well, not as marketing sticker
- **Vote for**: Deep integration where their primitive enables the product
- **Skeptical of**: "Powered by [our SDK]" without core dependency

### Group 5: VCs / Investors (Foundation Capital, Kosmos, Underdog founder)
- **Care about**: Investability — TAM, defensibility, team, monetization
- **Vote for**: Clear path to $100M+ business, strong founder story
- **Skeptical of**: No revenue model, weak market sizing

### Group 6: Past Winners (Billy attn.markets, Justin Swig, Phil/Dom Kwok EasyA, etc.)
- **Care about**: Real execution vs theatrics
- **Vote for**: Actually-shipping products, not vapor demos
- **Skeptical of**: BS pitches, deck > demo, hand-wavy "we'll figure it out"
