## Global Stats (5,428 projects, 293 winners → 5.4% win rate)

### Solo founders WHO WON across hackathons:
- Capitola (Cypherpunk Consumer $25K) — solo
- MCPay (Cypherpunk Stablecoin $25K) — solo
- attn.markets (Cypherpunk Undefined $25K) — solo
- Pregame (Radar Consumer $30K) — solo
- Supersize (Radar Gaming $30K) — solo
- (likely more across other tracks)

**Conclusion**: Solo CAN win. Pattern: very tight focus + specific primitive + working demo. Solo doesn't disqualify, but you must execute sharper than 5-person teams.

## Repeating Patterns Across Hackathons

1. **Vertical-specific financial products** — BNPL (Yumi, Split Finance), Forex (FXSwap), Sports betting (Pregame, AlphaFC), Tax-efficient offramp (Quartz)
2. **Devtool / SDK / Framework** — Seer (debug), Txtx (runbooks), Verve (smart wallet infra), Riverboat (PM liquidity SDK)
3. **Hardware / Physical** — Unruggable (wallet), Cura (pet collar), SvachSakthi (energy)
4. **Aggregator / Meta-layer** — Capitola (PM aggregator), Squeeze (long/short routing)
5. **Novel primitive on top of new tech** — MCPay (MCP+x402), Verve (zk compression), Pythia (ICM)
6. **Mobile-native consumer** — Trepa (sentiment), Pregame (sports), Marshmallow (literacy)
7. **Riding sponsor tech** — Melee Markets (Arcium), Pregame (Switchboard), Supersize (MagicBlock), Verve (Light Protocol)

## What Lost

- Generic "decentralized X on Solana" (X = anything)
- Polymarket/PumpFun clones without novel mechanism
- Telegram payment bots (8 tried, 0 won) — need novel angle
- Splitwise on Solana (5 tried, 0 won)
- Geographic-vertical alone (dbirr Africa, Prediksi.lol Indonesia — both lost)
